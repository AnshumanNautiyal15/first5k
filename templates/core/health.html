Health & safety

{{ SITE_NAME }} offers general, gentle exercise guidance for healthy adults. It is not medical advice and doesn't replace advice from a doctor who knows you.

Check with a doctor before starting if…

  • you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, asthma or another long-term condition
  • you've ever had chest pain, fainting or dizziness, especially during activity
  • you have bone, joint or back problems that could get worse with exercise
  • you're pregnant or recently gave birth
  • you take medicine for your heart or blood pressure
  • you're under 18, or you're simply unsure

Stop straight away and get help if you feel…

  • pain, pressure or tightness in your chest, arm, neck or jaw
  • very short of breath, dizzy, faint or confused
  • a sharp or sudden pain anywhere

In an emergency call your local emergency number (112 in India and the EU, 911 in the US, 999 in the UK).

Everyday tips

  • The talk test: you should be able to hold a conversation while you jog. If you can't, slow down.
  • Walking is always allowed. There's no such thing as too slow.
  • Some muscle soreness a day or two after running is normal. Pain that gets worse while you run is not; stop and rest.
  • Drink water, wear comfortable shoes, and avoid the hottest part of the day.

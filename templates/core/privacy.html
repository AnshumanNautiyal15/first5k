Privacy policy

{{ SITE_NAME }} is run by {{ OPERATOR_NAME }}. We collect as little as we can, and only to run your plan.

What we store

  • Your email address, to log you in and send your plan emails.
  • Your onboarding answers: how active you are, whether you can jog for a minute, and your age group. These set your starting level and how quickly the plan progresses. We don't ask for your name, exact age, gender or location.
  • Your plan and progress: blocks, which days you marked done, how each block felt, milestones.
  • Your timezone, so unlock times and emails fit your day.
  • A record of the emails we sent you, so we never send the same one twice.
  • Login link requests, including the IP address, to stop abuse. Old ones are deleted automatically after 30 days.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't use passwords, so there's no password to leak.
  • We only use cookies needed to keep you logged in and protect forms.{% if PLAUSIBLE_DOMAIN %} We use Plausible, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that doesn't use cookies or track you across sites.{% endif %}

Who processes data for us

Our hosting provider stores the database, and our email provider delivers emails. They only process data to provide those services.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from plan emails with one click in any email, or from your account page.
  • Download your data as a file from your account page.
  • Delete your account from your account page. Your account and plan are deleted immediately and permanently.
